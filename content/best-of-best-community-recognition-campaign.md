# 2026 Best of the Best Community Recognition Campaign

## Purpose and Positioning

This campaign acknowledges the readers who recognized Morrison Insurance as **Best Insurance Agency** and Monica Watson as **Best Insurance Agent** in The Light and Champion’s 2026 Shelby County Readers Choice Best of the Best Awards. It is a **community thank-you**, not a victory lap. Each announcement stands on its own, thanks the people who voted, and makes room to congratulate other Shelby County winners.

> **Campaign principle:** Let the recognition be visible, but let gratitude carry the message.

The official award mark should be used exactly as supplied. Do not add vote totals, "most voted" claims, comparative claims, or a new claim beyond the award’s official name. The available research confirms the program identity and Monica Watson’s category through a public local winners listing; the agency award is confirmed by the agency owner, with publisher-issued category proof still to be added to campaign records when available. [1] [2]

## Website Treatment

The homepage now includes a compact, permanent acknowledgement between the customer-testimonial and carrier sections. It uses the official 2026 Best of the Best mark with the message **“Thank you, Shelby County.”** The treatment names both recognitions and congratulates fellow local winners without disrupting the site’s community-first hero or adding a sales call to action.

## Scheduling Rule

All social placements remain subject to the existing **three-post maximum per day across all networks**. Before selecting any date below, check Metricool’s total scheduled count for that date. A Facebook and Instagram pair consumes two of the three available daily positions; if two or more posts already exist, move the pair to the next suitable date with fewer than two scheduled posts.

## Stand-Alone Announcement Sequence

| Post | Timing window | Purpose | Primary channels | Visual direction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Reader Thank-You | Next available 1–2 week window | Thank the people who voted and set a humble tone | Facebook + Instagram | Official award mark paired with a real Morrison building or team photo; warm, simple, no confetti graphic |
| 2. Fellow-Winner Congratulations | 2–3 weeks after Post 1 | Celebrate the broader Shelby County business community | Facebook + Instagram | Authentic Center/Shelby County streetscape or a local-business-window montage; no brand favoritism |
| 3. Agency Recognition | 4–6 weeks after Post 1 | Explain what the Best Insurance Agency recognition means in daily service | Facebook + Instagram; LinkedIn manual | Real office or service-in-action photo, with a small official award mark only |
| 4. Monica Watson Recognition | 6–8 weeks after Post 1 | Recognize Monica without making the agency the hero | Facebook + Instagram; LinkedIn manual | Candid, authentic photo of Monica helping a neighbor or working in the office; no headshot-forward layout |
| 5. Service Follow-Through | 10–12 weeks after Post 1 | Gently close the fall run with a service-focused reminder | Facebook + Instagram | Ordinary, useful local-service moment; optional small award mark in a corner |

After the initial 3–4 month sequence, award language may appear **no more than quarterly** and only when it naturally supports a helpful community or service post.

## LinkedIn Version

Hold all LinkedIn uses until the page has the established minimum follower threshold. When used, lead with the community rather than the award: **“Shelby County readers recognized Morrison Insurance and Monica Watson in The Light and Champion’s 2026 Best of the Best Awards. We are grateful to serve alongside so many local businesses and people who make this county stronger.”** Keep the post under 120 words and pair it with the official mark plus an authentic local image.

## Google Business Profile Guidance

Use **one** GBP Update only, after a capacity check and with a real photo of the Morrison Insurance building or a recognizable Center/Shelby County location—not the award graphic. Keep it informational, plain-text, and free of URLs or phone numbers in the caption.

**Suggested GBP caption:**

Shelby County readers recently recognized Morrison Insurance as Best Insurance Agency and Monica Watson as Best Insurance Agent in The Light and Champion’s 2026 Best of the Best Awards. We appreciate the neighbors who voted and the local businesses and people recognized alongside us. Community recognition is a reminder to keep answering questions clearly, treating people kindly, and showing up when it matters. Morrison Insurance has served Shelby County since 1923.

Attach a **Learn More** button to the homepage; do not paste the URL into the caption.

## Reusable Language for Future Content

Use one of these short lines only when it fits naturally; do not place an award reference in every post.

| Context | Approved language |
|---|---|
| Footer or image caption | Recognized by Shelby County readers in The Light and Champion’s 2026 Best of the Best Awards. |
| Helpful local post | Proud to serve the neighbors who recognized us in the 2026 Best of the Best Awards. |
| Monica-related service post | Monica Watson was recognized as Best Insurance Agent by Shelby County readers in 2026. |
| Community partnership | Grateful to be recognized alongside the people and businesses that keep Shelby County strong. |

Avoid **“#1,” “most trusted,” “most voted,” “the county’s favorite,”** vote totals, or language that turns the recognition into a sales pitch.

## Brand Kit Guidance

Use the official award mark at no more than 14–18% of the visual canvas. Pair it with real Morrison Insurance or Shelby County photography, a cream or warm-neutral background, and the existing Morrison serif/Inter typographic system. Avoid confetti, trophies, inflated gold effects, stock handshakes, or a headshot-forward agent design.
