# Weekly Social Media Wrap-Up

This is the review-first handoff that turns the active weekday Content Desk into a usable Morrison Insurance social calendar. It is a single weekly package rather than a collection of uncoordinated posts. Every proposed item must identify its source, image direction, platform copy, and an eligible date verified to have fewer than three posts already scheduled in Metricool.

## Required weekly sources

| Content source | Content Desk day | Required social deliverable | Publishing guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Material weather, burn-ban, wildfire, flood, evacuation, or comparable safety information | Weekday monitor | A PSA only when the alert is timely and materially useful | Never create a routine weather post; check Metricool before suggesting a date. |
| GSC evidence and local community information | Monday | One SEO-led editorial recommendation and one timely community-post concept | Do not make a ranking claim without the current GSC report; no publishing without approval. |
| Center, Joaquin, Tenaha, Timpson, and Shelbyville athletics | Tuesday | Carousel-ready school details plus neutral county-wide Facebook and Instagram captions | Cover the sport with the strongest current local relevance; do not include Martinsville in Shelby County messaging. |
| Toledo Bend fishing conditions | Thursday | A Canva fishing-template fill-in, a Facebook caption, an Instagram caption, sources, and image direction | Keep the report factual and community-serving; recommend timing only after checking the three-post daily cap. |

## Thursday Toledo Bend fishing handoff

The Toledo Bend report is an explicit part of the weekly social-media wrap-up. The Thursday output must include current lake conditions, active species and techniques, moon/timing information, a brief outlook rationale, the fill-in for the approved fishing template, and platform-ready Facebook and Instagram captions. Its community value comes first; it should not carry an insurance pitch.

The report is prepared for review and scheduling. It does not post automatically. Once Morrison Insurance approves it, confirm Metricool capacity and schedule it for an eligible Thursday morning window, normally 8:00–9:00 AM CDT.
