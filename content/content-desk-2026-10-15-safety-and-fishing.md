## 1. Material safety development: Fire Weather Watch for Shelby County

The National Weather Service has issued a **Fire Weather Watch** for Shelby County from **Friday morning, October 16, through late Friday night**. The watch calls for north winds of 10–20 mph with gusts up to 30 mph and relative humidity of 25–40%. The NWS says fires that develop could spread rapidly, recommends against outdoor burning, and advises avoiding activities that could cause sparks. [1]

This is a weather watch, not a new Shelby County burn ban. Shelby County Today reported on October 9 that Commissioners Court lifted the burn ban, while urging careful, attended controlled burning. The Texas A&M Forest Service’s October 13 list still names Shelby among Texas counties with burn bans, so the official legal burn-ban status is not sufficiently reconciled for this handoff. Do **not** say that burning is legal or illegal in the social copy. Follow the Fire Weather Watch’s clear guidance: avoid outdoor burning and spark-producing work, then check for updates. ## 2. Toledo Bend lake note — review-ready social materials

### Current conditions

Toledo Bend Reservoir is **85.2% full** as of October 15, with a mean water level of **167.98 feet**—about **4 feet below** its 172-foot conservation-pool elevation. The Texas Water Development Board’s current reading is provisional and subject to revision. [4]

The Sabine River Authority of Louisiana lists both generators as **not running** and spillway gates as **closed**. It notes that the road and recreation area below the spillway close when gates are open; no such closure is presently shown. [5]

A current public, species-specific bite report was not available. Texas Parks and Wildlife says its weekly fishing reports are currently on hold while it updates its reporting method. A current Toledo Bend guide report dated October 8 is available only to paying members, so its catch and technique details were not used. [6] [7]

There is one timely, verified bass note: on October 6, a 10.74-pound bass was weighed, tagged, and released at Holly Park Marina. That is a single reported catch, not a lakewide bite forecast. [8]

The NWS forecast calls for much cooler and windy conditions on Friday, with north winds 5–15 mph and gusts up to 25 mph near the lake. [9]

### Upcoming access and tournament note

A Bassmaster Elite Qualifier is listed for Toledo Bend Reservoir in Many, Louisiana, from **October 21–23**, targeting bass. Confirm any launch or access changes directly with the organizer before travelling. [10]
