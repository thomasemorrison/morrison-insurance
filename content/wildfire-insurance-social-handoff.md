## Facebook Caption

Dry weather is a good time to do a little ordinary property upkeep and take a calm look at your homeowners policy.

Fire-related damage is commonly included in many homeowners policies, but the details still matter. Your deductible, home limit, other structures, belongings, and temporary-living coverage all come from the policy tied to your address.

Our new East Texas checklist walks through a few practical places to start: save a current home inventory, clear leaves and pine straw around structures, think through sheds and workshops, and keep a copy of your declarations page where you can reach it.

For homes, farms, and ranches around Shelby County, the goal is simply to know what you have before you ever need it. Read the guide in our Resources section.
