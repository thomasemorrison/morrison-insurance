## Authentic 4:5 carousel direction

Create a **five-card, portrait 4:5 carousel (1080 × 1350 px)** with one school per card, in the stated rotation. Use a warm, documentary-style background photo treatment that feels recognizably East Texas—such as a softly blurred field, track, court texture, or stadium lights—but do not present a photo as a live image of a specific game unless it is licensed and accurately captioned. Keep the design neutral: a restrained navy, charcoal, off-white, and muted field-green palette; plain sans-serif type; no mascots as a dominant visual; no athlete ranking, rivalry language, or team preference.

Each card should place the **school name and a small “Community Sports Desk” label** at the top, then group events by date with prominent sport, opponent/meet, time, and location. Preserve **“Not listed,” “TBD,”** and the relevant conflict note in smaller but readable text. Use a thin source line or URL/QR-area at the bottom that corresponds to the source column above. If the safety item is produced while active, make it a **separate, single 4:5 card**: high-contrast heat advisory headline, “Shelby County,” advisory end time, practical hydration/shade/check-on-neighbors guidance, NWS attribution, and no wildfire or burn-ban claim.
