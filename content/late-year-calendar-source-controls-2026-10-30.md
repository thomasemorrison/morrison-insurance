# Morrison Insurance — November–December Calendar Source Controls

This file preserves the current, external evidence and publishing constraints used to build the late-year Facebook, Instagram, Google Business Profile, and LinkedIn material library. It is not itself a posting queue.

## Core official and primary sources

- [NWS Shelby County active alerts](https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active.atom?zone=TXZ153) — use for new weather-warning eligibility; recheck every time-sensitive claim on publishing day.
- [Shelby County official public notices](https://www.co.shelby.tx.us/page/shelby.Public.Notices) — use for official county orders, including burn-ban issuance, extension, and rescission.
- [Shelby County burn-ban orders](https://www.co.shelby.tx.us/upload/page/2731/docs/Burn%20Ban%20Order.pdf) — confirm the effective date, scope, and whether a newer order supersedes it.
- [Texas A&M Forest Service burn-ban list](https://tfsfrp.tamu.edu/WILDFIRES/BURNBAN.txt) — corroborating county-status check, not a substitute for the county order.
- [TPWD 2026–27 hunting season dates](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/hunting/2026-2027-hunting-season-dates) and [Shelby County regulations](https://tpwd.texas.gov/regulations/outdoor-annual/regs/counties/shelby) — confirm current county, species, zone, license, and rule applicability before any outdoors post.
- [Texas Department of Transportation holiday-season guidance](https://www.txdot.gov/safety/traffic-safety-campaigns/holiday-season.html) and [current road conditions](https://conditions.drivetexas.org/current/) — use only for current-condition or general safe-driving messages; never imply a local enforcement operation or crash trend without a separate official source.
- [NWS winter preparedness](https://www.weather.gov/safety/winter) and [Ready.gov winter weather](https://www.ready.gov/winter-weather) — general preparation only, never a local forecast.
- [Texas A&M AgriLife fall vegetable guide](https://agrilifeextension.tamu.edu/browse/featured-solutions/gardening-landscaping/fall-vegetable-gardening-guide-for-texas/) — regional context only; advise readers to consult local Extension guidance for timing.
- [Shelby County Chamber events](https://shelbycountychamber.com/chamber-events/) — current verified anchor for December 3 Annual Taste of the Holidays at John D. Windham Civic Center and December 5 Santa’s Lighted Christmas Parade on the historic Center square. The source did not publish start times; reconfirm those and all operational details on posting day.
- [Fannie Brown Booth Memorial Library](https://www.centerlibrary.org/) — verify current events, dates, and images before publishing.
- [Texas Parks and Wildlife Toledo Bend report](https://tpwd.texas.gov/fishboat/fish/action/reptform2.php?lake=TOLEDO+BEND&yearcat=current&archive=byweek&Submit=Go), [Texas Water Development Board lake data](https://waterdatafortexas.org/reservoirs/individual/toledo-bend), and [Sabine River Authority](https://srala-toledo.com/) — use for the weekly lake post.

## Mandatory controls

1. Do not invent a local event, start time, organizer, ticket information, route, closure, school activity, score, hunting rule, forecast, market figure, or charity need.
2. Recheck all time-sensitive claims on the posting day; alert, road, event, and outdoor-regulation conditions can change.
3. Keep every item community-first: no products, prices, coverage implications, fear tactics, claim outcomes, or required-action implication.
4. GBP copy must contain neither a URL nor a phone number and must use a real Morrison or recognizable local photograph. LinkedIn stays professional and community-facing; it is not a replica of Facebook copy.
5. The late-year exception permits an elevated calendar but does not excuse duplication or filler. Each item needs a distinct useful point, a low-friction response prompt, and authentic/rights-cleared visual direction.
