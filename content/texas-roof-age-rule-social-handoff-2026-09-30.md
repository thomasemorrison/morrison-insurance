## Source guardrails

- TDI says a company cannot decline or nonrenew a residential property policy solely because a house or roof is older.
- TDI also says a company may consider the physical condition of the property or its components.
- The September 2026 proposal clarifies an existing rule; TDI says it does not change the rule’s meaning or application.

Can an older roof automatically keep a Texas homeowner from getting or renewing a policy?

Not by age alone. Texas insurance rules say a company cannot make that decision solely because a home or roof is older. A company can still consider the physical condition of the roof and other parts of the home.

That difference matters. A renewal notice or inspection request may be asking about the roof’s condition, documentation, or repairs—not simply the year it was installed.
