# Morrison Insurance — Automated Safety and Toledo Bend Publishing Standard

**Authorized by Morrison team:** October 30, 2026  
**Applies through:** Until revised by the Morrison team  
**Destinations:** Facebook and Instagram only  
**Not authorized:** Google Business Profile automation, LinkedIn automation, automatic email, any Pack of Strays delivery, website changes, paid-media changes, or new standalone recurring content tasks.

## Purpose

This limited automation exists to make genuinely useful, time-sensitive East Texas information available without turning Morrison’s social presence into a generic alert feed or a sales channel. It is an exception to the normal review-ready content process and must stay community-first, factual, calm, and locally relevant.

## 1. NWS Shelby Safety Publisher — active

**Official source:** [NWS Shelby County alert Atom feed](https://api.weather.gov/alerts/active.atom?zone=TXZ153)  
**Check cadence:** Native feed checks approximately every 10 minutes.  
**Eligible automatic posts:** A new official NWS alert that clearly applies to Shelby County/Center and is a Tornado Warning, Severe Thunderstorm Warning, Flash Flood Warning, Flood Warning, Winter Storm Warning, Ice Storm Warning, Red Flag Warning, Extreme Heat Warning, or an NWS emergency message requiring urgent protective action.

The publisher must use the NWS entry and linked alert as the source of truth, publish only once per new eligible alert, and include the official source link. It must not auto-post watches, advisories, special weather statements, routine forecasts, expired/cancelled alerts, unverified Shelby County applicability, or duplicate/unchanged information.

**Creative and copy:** Use the approved real Morrison building JPEG, plain language, the official alert end time when available, and only concrete NWS-recommended actions. No insurance-product language, price or claim language, fear tactics, phone numbers, or sales call to action. Use restrained local hashtags only when they help people find the update.

A qualifying urgent update is authorized even if routine calendar content has already filled the day. It does not edit or cancel any existing placement.

## 2. Weekly Toledo Bend fishing post — active

**Schedule:** Every Thursday at 10:00 AM America/Chicago.  
**Destinations:** Facebook and Instagram.

The Thursday Content Desk research must use current authoritative Toledo Bend sources before scheduling the paired post. Useful sources include Texas Parks and Wildlife, Texas Water Development Board, Sabine River Authority, an official tournament organizer, and a current public guide report when available.

When a current source supports species or technique language, use it plainly and attribute it. When a supportable bite report is not available, publish a dated lake-conditions note instead of inventing a forecast. The post must use a public, rights-cleared, recognizable Toledo Bend/Deep East Texas image and accurate alt text. If no valid source or image can be confirmed, it must skip publication and record the exact blocker in the Unified Content Desk thread.

The fishing post may run even if routine calendar placements already exist. It does not automatically publish to GBP or LinkedIn.

## 3. Burn-ban changes — not yet automated

Burn-ban changes are materially important to Shelby County and should be acted on promptly. At present, there is no discovered native official county change event. A scheduled monitor would need explicit separate approval for its frequency and official page/PDF scope before it can be activated. Until then, the Unified Content Desk prepares an immediate review-ready Facebook/Instagram post from the official Shelby County order or rescission notice.

## Audit and safeguard record

Each automation execution must record the triggering source, decision, source link, publication time, selected image, Metricool result or planner URL, and any failed-precondition/blocker. Any provider authorization error, source outage, or invalid publication result must be surfaced rather than retried blindly.
