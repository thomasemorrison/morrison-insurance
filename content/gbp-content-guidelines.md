# Google Business Profile — Content Guidelines for Morrison Insurance

These rules are based on observed post rejections. Apply them to every GBP post before scheduling.

## What Gets Posts Rejected

**URLs in body text.** GBP rejects posts that contain a URL typed into the post copy. The destination link must go in the dedicated Button field only (Learn More, Call, etc.). Never include `https://` or any web address in the post text itself.

**Fear-based or urgency-driven language.** Posts that imply the reader may be at risk or unprotected are flagged, especially from financial services businesses. Avoid:
- "Do you know if you're covered?"
- "Are you protected?"
- "Hurricane season is here — are you ready?"
- Any question that implies a coverage gap

**Promotional insurance phrases.** GBP flags these as advertising language from regulated financial services accounts:
- "We've got you covered"
- "Get covered"
- "Protect your [home/family/farm]"
- Leading with "coverage" as the subject

**Weather-as-warning framing.** Seasonal weather posts that read as risk warnings (e.g., "We're in the heart of hurricane season") are rejected. Weather content is fine when framed as community information, not as a prompt to buy insurance.

---

## What Works on GBP

- Community updates and local information (events, weather as news, seasonal tips)
- Plain-language educational content that doesn't imply a coverage gap
- Posts that lead with the reader's situation or local context, not insurance
- Helpful tips framed as general knowledge, not as a sales prompt
- The button field handles the CTA — the post body should inform, not sell

## Post Structure That Passes

1. **Body text:** Local/seasonal/educational content. No URLs. No fear language. No promotional phrases.
2. **Button:** Set to "Learn More" or "Call" with the destination URL entered in the button field only.
3. **Image:** Optional but improves visibility. Use community-appropriate photos, not stock insurance imagery.
