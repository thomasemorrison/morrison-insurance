[2] Texas A&M Forest Service’s latest retrieved live text listing, timestamped **September 21, 2026, 12:53:10 PM**, includes SHELBY among counties under burn bans. [3]

Status may change after the listed check times; confirm the county notice again immediately before any posting, response, or community guidance.
