# Deferred Educator Outreach Plan

## Purpose

Offer Shelby County educators a **free, plain-language insurance review** without scraping teacher contact information, implying unverified discounts, or sending unsolicited mass messages.

## Recommended sequence

1. **Create an opt-in landing page or form.** Use an educational title such as “A back-to-school insurance review for Shelby County educators.” Explain that the review is a chance to compare current information and ask questions; do not promise a lower premium or an educator discount.
2. **Collect only voluntary information.** Limit the form to name, preferred contact method, school district (optional), and permission to follow up. Include a clear privacy note and consent checkbox for marketing follow-up.
3. **Use compliant paid social.** Target the five Shelby County school communities geographically, with broad local education/back-to-school interests where available. Do not compile or upload teacher email lists gathered from public directories. Keep the creative helpful and local, not product-led or fear-based.
4. **Route opt-ins into HubSpot.** Create an `Educator Review Interest` list, attach the form submission date and consent source, then send a brief confirmation and an optional invitation to choose a review time.
5. **Use Messenger only for inbound conversations.** Reply promptly when someone messages the agency from an ad or post. Do not initiate mass Messenger outreach to individuals or school groups.
6. **Verify any savings claim before mentioning it.** Carrier eligibility, availability, and pricing vary. If a carrier offers an educator-specific benefit, confirm the current terms before referring to it in any ad, landing page, email, or reply.

## Suggested neutral campaign language

> Educators keep Shelby County moving. If you would like a straightforward review of the insurance information you already have, Morrison Insurance is available to help you compare your options and ask questions without the runaround.

## Success measures

Track opt-in form completions, scheduled review conversations, contact permission rate, and any voluntarily disclosed school district. Do not use raw public contact lists as a campaign metric or source.
