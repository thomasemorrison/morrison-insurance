## Verified Texas safety points

TxDOT advises drivers to obey active school-zone speed limits, keep phones out of reach, yield to people in crosswalks, use designated pickup areas, and watch for children at bus stops or between parked vehicles. Texas drivers must not pass a school bus displaying flashing red lights or an extended stop sign, unless the bus is on the opposite side of a divided highway. TxDOT notes that a first stop-arm violation may bring a fine of up to $1,250.[^txdot-bus]

[^txdot-bus]: [TxDOT — School bus safety](https://www.txdot.gov/safety/driving-laws/school-bus-safety.html); [TxDOT — Back to School](https://www.txdot.gov/safety/traffic-safety-campaigns/back-to-school.html).
