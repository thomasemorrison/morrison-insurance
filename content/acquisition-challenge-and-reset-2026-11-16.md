# Morrison: useful content is not yet a strong acquisition program

**No—we are not yet doing everything that could realistically help Morrison earn attention and inquiries online.** The work has become consistent and well sourced, but consistency is not the same as discovery, and discovery is not the same as a new customer conversation. Increasing article or post counts without changing the approach would risk producing more of the same weak results.

Organic trust remains worth building. The weakness is that much of the executed content is interchangeable information, while too little demonstrates Morrison's particular local judgment and everyday help. Distribution is also underdeveloped. On the website, useful resources need a clearer connection to choosing a local agent. The first weekly growth priority—verifying inquiry receipt and measurement—still matters, but that alone will not create demand.

This is an evidence-backed recommendation, not a live change. No existing post was canceled, no new content was scheduled, and no website, tracking, paid-media or recurring-workflow setting was changed. Prepared by **Manus AI, November 16, 2026**.

## Search traffic is genuinely small; the “page two” headlines are too confident

The new supplied Search Console report shows **one click from 643 impressions**, displayed CTR of **0.2%**, and average position **38.9** over its stated last 28 days. That is very little Google organic acquisition. Last week's supplied snapshot showed two clicks and 613 impressions. These are overlapping, incompletely dated windows with tiny click counts; they do not establish a reliable decline or explain its cause. [1]

The opportunity labels also overstate what is known. “Homeowners insurance,” “monica watson” and “morrison agency” each have just one impression. The adjuster query has seven; the business query has two. An average position of 11 from one impression is not evidence that Morrison consistently occupies the top of page two. Google explains that position varies across searches, locations and result types; position 11 is not universally a page-two result. [2]

That changes the editorial decision. A claims question can be valuable customer service without being the best source of new shopping demand. We should not make another article simply because the notification calls it an opportunity. Require the query-to-page mapping, current demand evidence and an appropriate buyer need before choosing another SEO build.

## More social activity has not translated into comparable discovery

For **October 19–November 15**, Metricool reports 21 Facebook posts versus 12 in the preceding non-overlapping 28-day period. Facebook followers rose from 350 to 417, with 67 reported net additions versus 16 previously. That is a positive sign—not a program with no response at all—but follower changes do not identify their cause or confirm a local audience. [1]

The more revealing result is **five reported link clicks across 21 Facebook posts**, with median post reach **46**. The agency's November 7 voter thank-you reached **1,161** and received 54 reactions and seven comments. Three of the five link clicks came from that post, whose link points to The Light and Champion—not Morrison's website. The mean reach of roughly 145 therefore makes the ordinary post look stronger than the median suggests. We cannot call five link clicks five website visits, much less five leads. [1] [4]

The returned Instagram feed dataset has **21 posts**, median reach **nine**, no reported comments and no post-attributed follows. It does show **37 saves**, so some material is being retained. Shares are unavailable in this response, not proven zero. The account-level evolution endpoint reports 22 posts and one Reel, while the feed endpoint returns 20 single images and one carousel; we cannot conclude there were no Reels anywhere. What is clear is that the measured feed program is overwhelmingly static. The follower endpoint moved from 31 to 35, which is growth but still a very small audience. [1] [5]

## Keep community usefulness, but make Morrison's expertise visible

School schedules, fishing conditions and garden guidance are worth keeping when they help neighbors. They should not be forced into insurance pitches. But a reader can get similar information from schools, lake guides, Extension or local publishers. Accurate summaries alone do not explain why that reader should remember Morrison when a renewal or business question arises. [8]

The best-performing thank-you used real people, real local recognition and a familiar relationship. That is evidence in favor of recognizable, human material—not permission to turn awards into an ongoing promotional series. The existing limited award exception remains limited. We need to carry the authentic quality into ordinary useful content.

For insurance education, change the unit of production from **“an article plus a generic image”** to **“one real question, a practical demonstration and one easy next step.”** These are candidate treatments, not approved scripts:

- **“Renewal went up—what should you have ready before comparing?”** A real staff member explains three items using a clearly marked sample document, not a customer's policy. The point is a fair comparison, not a promise of savings.
- **“Does calling something a work truck answer how it's used?”** Show the questions an agent needs to ask about a pickup used for commuting and paid work. Do not assert a coverage result.
- **“A contractor asked for a certificate—what information do they need?”** Demonstrate a privacy-safe request checklist and explain why the certificate wording matters, without displaying a real customer's documents.

Lead with the reader's question and the demonstration, not a headshot or agency achievement. Use actual office/work scenes, caption the explanation, and keep the agency's appearance incidental. A person-led video is not automatically good; the opening has to make the useful question obvious.

The earlier creative review found a gap between the Reels, Stories and collaborations written into our plan and the executions established by the reviewed records. Original, useful video is a reasonable discovery test. Instagram's guidance supports original eligible content reaching new audiences, but eligibility does not guarantee recommendations; current Account Status still needs an authorized check. [8] [9] [10]

## Build distribution rather than relying on hashtags

For the next test, **replace formats within the existing 7–10 weekly placements per channel**. Do not raise the ceiling. Keep sports and the single Thursday fishing report. Try two short original explanations each week in place of weaker static education/promotional slots. Use a small number of purposeful Stories to ask or answer a real question, substituting them for other placements when needed because Stories also consume daily room.

Make the resource path easier. A caption that asks Instagram readers to type a domain, find Resources and locate a long title adds friction. Inspect the actual profile link, then propose a clear direct route to the featured useful resource and a Story link where supported. Do not claim the present bio is broken—it has not been checked. Facebook can use the exact live page; GBP needs its separate compliant button/photo handling.

Local distribution should also include **genuine, permission-based collaboration**. A useful feature with a local organization may expose Morrison to a relevant audience beyond its own followers. A tag alone is not a collaboration, and a private customer relationship is not permission to name that business publicly. Start with a real shared topic and an agreed contribution. Do not launch mass outreach, unsolicited customer posts or comment spam. Our possible partner ideas remain private.

Reply thoughtfully to actual comments and questions. Ask about the reader's situation rather than the packaging of our content. “What is hardest to gather before comparing your renewal?” is more useful audience research than “Would a checklist help?” Keep personal details out of public replies. Hashtags can clarify the topic, but there is no measured evidence here that changing hashtags will solve the distribution gap.

## Treat local search as a separate acquisition job

Articles do not substitute for a strong local business presence. Google's local ranking guidance centers on **relevance, distance and prominence**. Accurate categories and business information, genuine reviews, local references and links, and authentic photos deserve attention. We cannot change distance through publishing, and frequent GBP Updates are not identified as a direct ranking lever in that guidance. [11]

Propose a read-only GBP audit of categories, business details, website links, recent photos and review handling. Confirm address, hours and other listings against the agency's actual information rather than copying a directory. Use a fair, genuine review-request process after service, value balanced feedback and protect customer privacy in replies. No incentives, invented reviews or selective pressure for praise. No review outreach or profile change is activated by this report. [12]

There is also a reporting problem to resolve: the current Metricool GBP reach/action rows stop on **October 28**, although Facebook and Instagram continue through November 15. That makes the GBP 28-day decline comparison unusable. It is a data-coverage problem in this export, not proof that Google visibility or the profile itself failed. Obtain native GBP Performance for compatible dates before deciding how local search is doing. [1]

The public discovery review found Morrison through Progressive's agent directory rather than its own homepage/auto page in a small query sample. That is a useful concern, not an exact Google rank or proof of non-indexing. A geographically controlled local-rank check is still needed; competitor traffic, keyword difficulty and market share were not available, so no numerical competitor-gap or content-velocity claims are made here. ## Improve the shopping pages before producing another generic article

Morrison already has real local people, carrier evidence, attributed customer experiences and substantial auto guidance. The site does not need to imitate a captive carrier or replace its design. The opportunity is to make the existing proof and help easier to act on. [13]

The reviewed Shoop site makes different quote paths explicit. Sandy Cox separates claim/payment/ID-card tasks from new shopping. These are decision-path examples—not proof that either competitor converts better, and not a recommendation to copy their tone or service claims. Morrison already explains independent choice; simply repeating “we shop options” will not distinguish it. [14] [15]

After the first measurement review, the next focused website candidate should be **the existing auto-service page**, not another close-match Center auto article. A buildable refresh would:

1. Bring forward a short preparation section, with links to the existing quote checklist and comparison guide instead of repeating whole articles.
2. Explain how equivalent quotes are compared using a clearly labeled illustrative example reviewed by a real agency designee. Do not include fabricated prices or customer outcomes.
3. Clarify the everyday pickup-use questions and separate recreational-policy questions. Review categorical language such as claims that a coverage always pays or nothing will fall through the cracks.
4. State the next human step and the actual team handling the request after internal confirmation. Distinguish a new quote from an existing-customer service task; do not promise a response time that has not been agreed.

For the homepage, simplify competing invitations and repeated reassurance so a new visitor can choose a local conversation without navigating app promotion. Preserve the recognizable office and people. This is a later hypothesis, not an instruction to rebuild the homepage and auto page simultaneously. [13]

New editorial should earn its place with first-hand added value: a question the team repeatedly receives, a reviewed explanation that resolves it, and an original practical asset. Google specifically asks whether content provides substantial added value rather than summaries of other sources. A unique stock/AI illustration and local town names do not by themselves establish first-hand expertise. Use a named reviewer only after that person actually reviews the factual material. [16]

## A four-week reset with a decision at the end

**First week: establish trustworthy signals and prepare the first treatment.** Keep this morning's priority: inspect the saved HubSpot confirmation/receipt and current GA4/GTM definitions through authorized access. Verify a safe accepted-submission-to-agency-receipt method before any repair. The absence of a listener in site source does not prove HubSpot's receipt path is broken. Obtain current GSC query/page exports and compatible Analytics acquisition/landing-page data. Meanwhile, choose one real customer question for a staff demonstration; preparation need not wait for every report. [17]

**Second week: execute a small approved creative substitution.** Prepare two original short explanations, use direct useful-resource routes and a genuine audience question, and seek one suitable collaboration only after consent. Preserve community anchors. One primary website improvement follows the measurement finding or the reviewed auto-page brief—not both at once.

**Third week: repeat, rather than chasing one strong result.** Use comparable question types, record the outcomes at similar post ages and repeat the treatment that brought useful local attention. A single award post or one unusually strong Reel should not set the whole strategy. Keep market and safety reporting honest even when a more dramatic headline might attract attention.

**Fourth week: decide what to retain.** Compare post medians and repeated results within each channel, rather than averages dominated by one post. Retain formats that improve relevant discovery and useful action; reduce interchangeable static promotions that repeatedly fail their intended purpose. Sparse inquiries make this directional learning, not proof of causation or a guarantee that four weeks will establish long-term SEO success.

Use three distinct measures:

- **Discovery:** reported non-follower reach, follows and relevant local participation, where available. Total reach alone cannot prove East Texas audience acquisition.
- **Useful action:** saves, shares, meaningful questions and verified visits to the useful resource. Link taps and profile visits remain intermediate actions.
- **Business outcome:** distinct genuine and qualified local inquiries, separated from service requests, spam and repeat contacts; use a private receipt/source record. Anonymous totals are enough for reporting.

The immediate goal is not an invented lead target. It is an observable improvement in these signals and reliable lead attribution. After the receipt path and compatible baseline are established, set a numerical lead target with the agency. **Needed from Morrison:** authorized read-only GA4/GSC/HubSpot/GTM settings evidence or dated exports, anonymous received/qualified inquiry totals, an accountable factual reviewer, and permission/availability for real footage. Do not send passwords or customer records.
