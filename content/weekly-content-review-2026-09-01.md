### Draft Opening Copy for the Refresh

Living on a few acres in East Texas can mean more than a house and a driveway. There may be a barn, a shop, fencing, a tractor, tools, animals, a side-by-side, or a small operation that helps keep the place going. That is why it is worth asking where a homeowners policy may stop and where a farm policy may begin. The answer depends on the details of the property and how it is used, so the best starting point is a clear inventory and a plain conversation—not assumptions.
