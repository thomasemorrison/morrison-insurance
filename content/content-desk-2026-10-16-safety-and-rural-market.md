## Material safety update: Shelby County burn ban and Fire Weather Watch

Shelby County Judge Allison Harbison issued a declaration of disaster and an order prohibiting outdoor burning effective October 15. The order prohibits **outdoor burning in the unincorporated areas of Shelby County** and takes effect immediately. [1]

The National Weather Service’s Fire Weather Watch remains in effect from Friday morning through late Friday night for Shelby County and surrounding East Texas counties. The forecast calls for north winds of 10–20 mph with gusts up to 30 mph, relative humidity between 25% and 40%, critically dry fuels, and a risk that any fire that starts could spread rapidly. The NWS advises following all fire restrictions and avoiding activities that could cause sparks. [2] [3]

This is a current, material safety development. The county order and weather watch support one concise, same-day community-safety post. This is not an insurance promotion.
