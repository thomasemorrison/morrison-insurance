**Review date:** October 14, 2026  
**Status:** Review-ready only. No post was published or scheduled.

## Verified public-safety development

A boil-water notice remains in effect **until further notice** for specified N.E.W. Water Supply Corporation customers. The notice was published October 12 and says electrical issues prompted the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality to require the notice. It applies to customers on **FM 711, CR 202, CR 203, CR 205, CR 223, Harmony, Fountain Town, Kellyville, and Woodland Acres communities**—not to all Shelby County residents. [1]

For drinking, cooking, and making ice, the notice directs affected customers to bring water to a vigorous rolling boil for **two minutes** and let it cool, or use bottled water or another suitable source. It says the water system will issue a separate rescission once the notice is no longer necessary. [1]

A direct N.E.W. Water Supply Corporation notice or rescission page was not available in current search results; this package relies on the public notice carried by Shelby County Today. **Recheck for a rescission immediately before any approval or publication.** [1]
