# Best of the Best 2026 — Social Scheduling Packet

This packet turns the award recognition into **four separate, community-first posts**. Each should be scheduled manually only after Metricool confirms the intended date has capacity. The series is deliberately paced so the recognition remains visible without taking over the calendar.

> **Scheduling gate:** A Facebook + Instagram pair consumes two daily post positions. Use the next date with **fewer than two** existing posts across all networks. If there are already two or more, move to the next suitable date. Do not place two award posts within 14 days of one another.

## Series at a Glance

| Priority | Stand-alone post | Scheduling window | Channels | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reader Thank-You | First available capacity after the current August content run | Facebook + Instagram | Thank voters; establish the humble tone |
| 2 | Congratulations to Fellow Winners | 14–21 days after Post 1 | Facebook + Instagram | Celebrate the broader local business and service community |
| 3 | Agency Recognition | 14–21 days after Post 2 | Facebook + Instagram; LinkedIn later | Explain the everyday service behind the agency recognition |
| 4 | Monica Watson Recognition | 14–21 days after Post 3 | Facebook + Instagram; LinkedIn later | Celebrate Monica in a neighborly, non-headshot-forward way |

The four posts can therefore run over roughly **six to nine weeks**. The later “service follow-through” post in the master campaign should wait until a natural, useful fall service topic provides a reason to mention the recognition again.
