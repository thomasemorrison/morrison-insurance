## Suggested Recognition Formats

| Format | When to use it | Example direction |
|---|---|---|
| Comment or story share | A winner has made its own award post | A warm one- or two-sentence congratulations from Morrison Insurance |
| Stand-alone community spotlight | The winner has a real local story or clear public benefit | One photo, a simple congratulations, and the award category |
| Group congratulations | Several winners serve the same part of community life | A carefully limited school, volunteer, or local-business roundup |
| Community event tie-in | A winner is involved in a local event already being covered | Congratulate them naturally within useful event information |

## Editorial Guardrails

Each acknowledgement should lead with the fellow winner and their contribution—not Morrison Insurance. Keep award references factual, avoid comparing winners, and space these acknowledgements around existing content so the overall calendar remains useful and community-focused.
