# Morrison Insurance — Three-Month Social Materials Library

**Planning window:** October 19, 2026–January 17, 2027  
**Prepared:** October 7, 2026  
**Status:** Review-ready draft library only. Nothing in this document is scheduled, published, emailed, or sent outside Morrison Insurance.

## What this solves

This library gives Morrison **two strong, source-backed permanent-feed drafts per week for 13 weeks**—one community/local idea and one useful seasonal, rural, or household-education idea. It is intentionally a **material library, not an automatic posting calendar**. The Unified Content Desk continues to decide whether a current sports, fishing, rural-market, weather, or public-safety item should replace a planned post.

The work stays anchored in one simple question:

> Would a neighbor in Shelby County or Deep East Texas find this helpful, recognizable, or worth passing along even if they were not shopping for insurance today?

## Non-negotiable operating rules

1. **No automatic scheduling or publishing.** Every item needs current Morrison approval.
2. **Cross-network daily cap: three placements.** Check Metricool immediately before recommending a date or time. A Facebook-and-Instagram cross-post counts as **two placements**; adding Google Business Profile would count as a third.
3. **Fresh facts before use.** Event dates, hours, fees, seasons, regulations, weather, road conditions, water notices, organization needs, and holiday office status must be rechecked from the cited primary source near the proposed date—and same day where the topic can change quickly.
4. **Local, rights-cleared visuals only.** Use Morrison-owned photos, permission-cleared local photos, school/organizer-approved images, or original neutral photography. Never use stock handshakes, generic corporate creative, unapproved youth/event images, or unlicensed government/organizer artwork.
5. **GBP is separate.** A GBP candidate must be an informational **Update**, use a real Morrison/Center/Shelby County photo, contain no phone number or URL in the caption, and use an in-product button for a link or call. Do not reuse the Facebook/Instagram copy automatically.
6. **No pressure or promises.** Do not promise a price, policy outcome, claim result, protection level, response time, individual coverage conclusion, or sponsorship. Do not use fear tactics.
7. **Five-school, fishing, and rural-market lanes remain conditional.** Their current source checks can replace a planned item; they are not automatic weekly posts.

Before any legacy row is allowed to run, review it against the current direction:

- Does it lead with a real local question or community benefit?
- Is it still factually appropriate and seasonally useful?
- If it includes GBP, is it an informational Update with a real local image and **no URL or phone number in the caption**?
- Does the date stay within the three-placement cross-network cap?
- Does the creative avoid carrier-style graphics, fear language, stock imagery, and unsupported coverage statements?

This new material library is designed to **replace, not stack on top of**, legacy posts that fail those checks.
