## Safety decision — Shelby County burn ban remains in effect

Shelby County’s current order was issued October 23. It prohibits outdoor burning in the county’s unincorporated areas, except for activities carried out by Federal Certified Agencies. The order lasts no more than seven days unless the Commissioners Court continues or renews it. Shelby County and the Texas A&M Forest Service both list the burn ban as active. [1] [2]

The current Center forecast calls for light winds through Thursday. The point-alert feed returned no entries but carries an older embedded update timestamp, so the county’s current order—not that feed—is the controlling local safety information for this handoff. [3] [4]
