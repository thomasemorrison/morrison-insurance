No direct project-level OAuth connection can therefore be completed through the currently available interface.

The active **Weekly GSC Report** and its companion SEO dashboard are the approved evidence source for Monday editorial decisions until a direct connection becomes available. A weekly evidence package should include the current 28-day query and page performance, a prior-period comparison when available, and the metrics necessary to identify ranking movements and high-impression, low-CTR opportunities.

The weekday Content Desk must identify a missing report or unavailable dashboard data explicitly; it must not infer ranking trends or present a data-backed content recommendation without an accessible source.
