## Setup and operating rules

- **Community-first standard.** Every item is warm, plain-spoken, useful, nonpolitical, and non-salesy. It contains no insurance jargon, product language, price, coverage, claim, response-time, or outcome promise. No fear tactics or pressure-based CTA.
- **Cadence.** Each daily entry is one paired Facebook/Instagram placement. The temporary late-year exception permits **two pairs per day**; it does not authorize duplication, filler, automatic scheduling, or publication. Each pair includes a low-friction prompt and 3–5 local/subject hashtags.
- **Only narrow automatic-publication exceptions.** A **new qualifying official NWS Shelby County/Center warning** may use the approved Facebook/Instagram warning workflow. The **Thursday 10:00 AM Central Toledo Bend Facebook/Instagram item** may use the approved factual workflow only after its same-day source gate passes. No GBP, LinkedIn, email, website, or other item auto-publishes.
- **Conditional means conditional.** “If,” “only if,” and “conditional” items must be replaced or held when the live official source does not confirm them. Time-sensitive facts, local events, road/weather/alert information, outdoor rules, lake conditions, burn restrictions, charity needs, and hours require the stated posting-day gate. Never turn an old screenshot, search snippet, or unchanged status into news.
- **Visual and rights standard.** Use only Morrison-owned, original, organizer-approved, or documented permission-cleared imagery. Do not use stock-image direction, downloaded government/organizer art, unapproved AI art, identifiable children/patrons/clients, weapons, harvested animals, private-property identifiers, license plates, or unconsented community photos. GBP requires a real Morrison or recognizable local photo.
- **Website links.** Use only the verified, genuinely relevant resource options listed below. Add a link only when it directly answers the post’s question; never invent a URL or use a web link as a sales CTA.
- **Channel separation.** GBP Update body copy below contains no URL or phone number and is not Facebook copy. LinkedIn candidates are professional and community-oriented, must be posted manually unless the follower rule is formally changed, and are not Facebook duplicates.
