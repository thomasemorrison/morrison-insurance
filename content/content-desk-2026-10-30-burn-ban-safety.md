## Material safety decision — Shelby County burn ban remains active

Shelby County’s official website continues to display **“Burn Ban in Effect”** and links to the County Judge’s October 23 outdoor-burning order. The order prohibits outdoor burning in unincorporated Shelby County, except for Federal Certified Agencies, and lasts no more than seven days unless continued or renewed by Commissioners Court. The Texas A&M Forest Service’s current list still includes Shelby County, and Shelby County Today continues to identify the order as active. [1] [2] [3]

No official renewal or lift was found in the county’s current public-notices calendar. Because the October 23 order reaches the end of its stated seven-day period today unless renewed, this post is appropriate only today. Before any later use, recheck the county website for a renewal, continuation, or lift. The NWS point-alert feed returned no active alerts but carries an old timestamp, so it is not treated as an all-clear. [4]
