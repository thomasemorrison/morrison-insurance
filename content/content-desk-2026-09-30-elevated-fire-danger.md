## Decision

A narrowly scoped public-safety post is supported **today**.

The National Weather Service Shreveport issued a **Fire Danger Statement** covering East Texas, including Shelby County forecast zone TXZ153, from **10 AM to 8 PM Wednesday, September 30**. It says weather conditions will be favorable for wildfire growth, with critically dry fuels, afternoon relative humidity of 30–45%, sustained southeast winds of 10–15 mph, and gusts of 20–25 mph. It discourages outdoor burning and activities involving open sparks or flames. [1]

This is an **elevated fire-danger reminder**, not a report of an active Shelby County wildfire, evacuation, property loss, road closure, or Red Flag Warning.

## Important burn-ban status limit

The Shelby County homepage still displays a September 17 burn-ban notice and the Texas A&M Forest Service’s September 27 county list includes Shelby. However, the available signed September 17 county order says its local disaster declaration lasts no more than seven days unless continued or renewed by Commissioners Court. A September 30 Commissioners Court agenda includes an item to discuss and possibly act on the current burn ban, but an agenda is not proof of an action or a controlling current order. [2] [3] [4]

**Do not write “the burn ban is active,” “the ban was renewed,” “the ban was lifted,” or any municipal-wide restriction claim in this post.** Use the NWS Fire Danger Statement as the sole basis for today’s public copy. Recheck the Commissioners Court outcome before adding any county-order language later today.
