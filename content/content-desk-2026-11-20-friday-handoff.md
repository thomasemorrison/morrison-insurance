## Rural Ag Report: one useful woodland question

Texas A&M Forest Service starts stewardship planning with the owner’s goals and a description of current and desired land conditions. The practical adaptation here is to write one goal and one question before a conversation with a forester. This is preparation, not a requirement to complete a management plan before asking for help. The source is undated and was inspected November 20. [1]
