## 2. Deep East Texas Rural Market Note — cattle snapshot

### What is current and usable

The latest **USDA AMS Texas Weekly Cattle Auction Summary** was published **September 25, 2026** for sales during **September 20–26, 2026**. USDA reports that **steer and heifer calves sold $3.00 to $15.00 higher than the prior week**; yearlings were not well tested.

### Local context, not a price claim

Nacogdoches Livestock Exchange reported on September 25 that its September 24 sale had **891 head** and that feeder steers and heifers sold higher than the prior sale, while packer cows and bulls sold steady. This supports the local relevance of the statewide summary but does **not** make the USDA numbers a Shelby County quote or a price guarantee.
