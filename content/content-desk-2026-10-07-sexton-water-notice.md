## Material safety finding: N.E.W. Water boil-water notice

A notice dated **October 6, 2026** says the Texas Commission on Environmental Quality required N.E.W. Water Supply Corporation (PWS #2030034) to notify customers in the **Sexton Community, Hwy 87, FM 353, and FM 1279** to boil water before consumption because of a low chlorine residual at Plant 4.

The notice advises boiling water used for drinking, cooking, and ice making at a vigorous rolling boil for two minutes, then cooling it before use. It says the water system will issue a separate rescission notice when boiling is no longer necessary. A current web search located the original notice and **no rescission notice** for this affected area.

### Scope guardrail

This is a **localized water-system notice**, not a countywide water emergency. Do not say all of Shelby County, all of Center, or any unlisted neighborhood is affected. Do not add a phone number in the caption; the source notice contains system contacts for people who need a location-specific answer.
