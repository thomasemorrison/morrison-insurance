# A useful question → a real conversation

## Operating principle

This campaign is not a content-volume experiment. Its job is to help a Shelby County or Deep East Texas reader understand one situation, choose a low-friction path, and reach a local person. A saved checklist, phone tap or follower is a useful signal—not a confirmed new-business inquiry. Keep existing service/customer help distinct from acquisition when recording outcomes.

## Reply safely and helpfully

**General public question:** “That is a good question to bring to a local agent. The answer can depend on how the property or vehicle is used and the actual policy. Please keep policy numbers and documents out of the comments. You can call the Center office or use the Contact page to start with a general question.”

**Reader asks what to prepare:** “Start with the question you want answered. The free checklist is optional—you do not have to fill it out or upload anything to use it. If documents would help, ask the team how to share them privately.”

**Reader asks for a price or coverage answer in comments:** “We can’t determine the price or what applies from a comment. A private conversation is the right next step. Mention the general situation first, and ask how the team wants to receive any needed documents.”

**Existing customer or claim help:** “For an existing policy or claim question, contact the Center office or your insurer using the contact information on your policy. Let the team know it is existing-customer help rather than a new quote request. Ask about the next step and timing directly.”

Do not promise a reply deadline, quote completion, lower price, claim result or specific coverage. Do not ask for driver licenses, VINs, policy numbers, contracts or account details in comments/DM templates. Do not copy private replies into public marketing or this task. Emergency guidance does not replace official emergency services.

## Record the outcome, not personal details

Use an anonymous row for each genuine new-business inquiry: date; general topic(auto/home/rural/business); entry route(page/FB/IG/GBP/referral/unknown); confirmed conversation or accepted form; next-step agreed(yes/no/unknown); outcome pending/appropriate help/no fit; duplicate flag. No name, contact address, policy, financial or medical information belongs in the campaign scorecard.

Separate counts: relevant entry sessions if measured; phone/email/form **attempts**; accepted submissions if verified; real qualified new-business conversations; booked follow-ups if actually arranged. Do not add attempts and submissions together as leads or double-count a person who called after a form. Ask “What brought you to us?” during a real conversation; use unknown when the answer is unavailable.

## Measurement implementation gate

The current GA4/GSC access and form-success/consent checks remain open. This build does not modify GTM, GA4, Ads conversions, HubSpot behavior or billing and creates no synthetic leads. A safe tracking proposal is separate: a consent-respecting, non-PII contact-attempt event; a success-only form event from the actual supported HubSpot callback; and separate reporting for attempted contact versus accepted inquiry. Event definitions and deduplication must be approved/tested before relying on them. Never infer form success merely from visiting /thank-you.

Campaign URL tags may use `utm_source=facebook|instagram|google_business_profile`, `utm_medium=organic_social`, `utm_campaign=conversation_path_2026`, `utm_content=wN_topicN`. Facebook's link can be tagged after confirming the live destination. Instagram caption directs to the profile Resources/website link; do not assume a new bio link was installed. GBP URL is button-only after an individual preview check, not body text. This document defines options; no account link or attribution setting has changed.

## Weekly learning loop

Use the existing Monday Content Desk to identify the most useful reader question and compare measured entry/attempt/inquiry outcomes. Friday reports what actually shipped, confirmed results and one learning. Compare equivalent periods and post ages; retain unknowns. Keep a successful hook only if it leads to relevant questions or visits, not solely because it collects likes. Allow authentic staff-video and practical-card variants to replace a planned placement, never pile on additional posts.

The first real KPI is verified qualified new-business conversations attributed where possible to the pathway. There is no promised number of leads or growth rate. The six-week calendar is an operational plan, not proof this system works yet.
