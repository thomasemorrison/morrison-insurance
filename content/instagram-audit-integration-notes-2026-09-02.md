# Instagram Audit Integration Notes — September 2, 2026

## Referenced audit findings

The referenced Instagram review examined the account's 20 most recent posts from August 14 through September 2, 2026. It found modest interaction overall, with 54 likes and no comments across the sample. The strongest directional signals were local heritage, rural outdoor relevance, and familiar concrete scenarios rather than generic coverage promotion. The top examples were the Morrison 1923-history post, a hunting-land Reel, and a local truck scenario; distant coastal-property content had little interaction.

The audit recommends a local-utility-led approach: Shelby County sports, current events, public-safety notices, local people and places, farm and poultry, outdoor life, and useful preparedness guidance. It recommends a 90/10 value-to-offer balance, one simple engagement prompt per post, original local creative, and a sustainable baseline of three feed posts per week supported by Stories.
