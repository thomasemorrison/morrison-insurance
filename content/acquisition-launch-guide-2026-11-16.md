## The website tools

**Start Here** helps a visitor choose a renewal/quote, home/rural, small-business or existing-customer question. A call and email route are visible immediately. The visitor does not have to work through a checklist before making contact.

**Prepare for a Conversation** offers three optional checklists. The visitor can check items on the page, clear a checklist, print it, or open a one-page PDF without signing up. There are no text fields, document uploads or submission from this tool. It collects no personal information and does not issue a quote or change a policy.

**What to Expect** explains how to start with the general situation, ask what documents are needed and how to share them privately, and confirm the next step with the existing local team. It names the real team without assigning an unconfirmed case handler or promising a response time, price or coverage result.
