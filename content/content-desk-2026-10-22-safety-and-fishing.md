## Toledo Bend lake note — current, sourced facts

The latest Texas Water Development Board reading, dated **October 20**, places Toledo Bend at **84.9% full**, with a mean water level of **167.89 feet**. That is **4.11 feet below** the 172-foot conservation-pool elevation; the agency identifies these readings as provisional. [6]

The Sabine River Authority of Louisiana lists both generators as **No Run** and the spillway gates as **Closed**. It says the road and recreation area below the spillway are closed when the gates are open; it does not show that closure now. [7]

A 2026 public, lakewide TPWD bite report is not available. TPWD says its weekly fishing reports are on hold while it improves future reporting, so its newest visible Toledo Bend entry is from October 2025 and must not be presented as current. [8]

For seasonal context only, a September 29 Toledo Bend guide feature reports that October bass activity often shifts through creeks, grass lines, timber lines, channel bends, and deep drains, while crappie may improve in creeks, bridge-piling areas, and natural timber. It is a guide’s fall pattern—not a live lakewide forecast or a promise of results. [9]

The 2026 Nitro Boats Bassmaster Elite Qualifier is under way on Toledo Bend. Bassmaster’s Day 1 coverage was published October 21, so visitors should expect tournament activity around the lake and use normal courtesy at ramps and on the water. [10]
