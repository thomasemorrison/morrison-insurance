## Sourceable anchors

- **New Year’s Day:** January 1 is listed in the federal holiday schedule. Do not infer Morrison office hours, local closures, or customer availability from the federal schedule. [1]
- **Safe holiday travel:** TxDOT’s official holiday message is to arrange a sober ride ahead of time if someone may drink, or stay put until sober. It does not establish a local enforcement operation or crash trend for Shelby County. [2]
- **Safe celebration:** CPSC’s fireworks guidance says to use only legal consumer fireworks, follow label instructions, keep children away, keep water nearby, never use fireworks while impaired, light one at a time, move back, and never relight a dud. Local legality and any county burn restriction must be checked before mentioning fireworks as an activity. [3]
- **Outdoor and hunting context:** TPWD’s 2026–27 season page lists, among other seasons, East Texas squirrel season through February 28, 2027; the Shelby County page identifies Center as the county seat and lists current county-specific seasons and requirements. Do not simplify these pages into a blanket statement that a hunt is legal; species, zone, license, endorsement, land, and restrictions still matter. [4] [5]
- **Toledo Bend:** TPWD describes Toledo Bend as spanning Shelby, Sabine, and Newton counties and points users to current lake level, fishing report, regulations, and access information. The page supports a general lake-life preparation or “check the current report” idea, not a claim about current water, weather, bite, ramp, or access conditions. [6]
- **Winter household readiness:** Ready.gov recommends preparing for winter risks, keeping anything burnable at least three feet from heat sources, using space heaters directly in an outlet, keeping generators outdoors and away from windows, and checking vehicle/emergency supplies. [7]
- **Texas pipe preparation:** Texas Department of Insurance guidance includes removing hoses, wrapping exposed pipes, draining/turning off sprinkler systems, and following local instructions during a freeze. It also says coverage depends on the individual policy and circumstances; no social post should make a coverage conclusion. [8]
- **Weather and current conditions:** NWS winter guidance explains that winter storms can bring snow, sleet, and freezing rain and directs readers to current alerts and forecasts. Use the NWS Shelby County alert feed and local forecast on posting day; do not say a freeze, storm, or hazardous road is coming without current official support. [9] [10]
