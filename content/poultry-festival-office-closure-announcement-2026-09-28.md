# Morrison Insurance — East Texas Poultry Festival Office-Closure Announcement

**Prepared:** September 28, 2026  
**Status:** Review-ready only. Nothing has been published, scheduled, submitted to Google Business Profile, or changed on the website.

## Confirmed scope

- The **50th Annual East Texas Poultry Festival** is listed for **October 1–3, 2026** in Center. [1]
- A Shelby County Chamber of Commerce traffic notice says the entire Downtown Center Square will be closed to vehicle traffic during festival operations: **Austin Street, Shelbyville Street, San Augustine Street, and Nacogdoches Street**. No through traffic will be permitted on any side of the square. [2]
- **Morrison Insurance’s Center office will be closed Thursday and Friday, October 1–2, because parking will not be available.** This is the Morrison team’s confirmed business update.
- Agents remain available by **phone and email** during the office closure. No response-time promise is made.

## Communication guardrails

- Lead with the practical downtown-access information, then the office closure and availability.
- Do not suggest the office is permanently closed, that every downtown business is closed, or that roads beyond the four sides of the square are closed.
- Do not add road-closure hours, parking instructions, bag dimensions, detours, sponsor claims, festival attendance claims, or other logistics not confirmed by the Chamber.
- Do not make this an insurance promotion. It is a service and access update.
- **Metricool is disabled:** no date, time, schedule, or daily-capacity conclusion is included. Before any placement is proposed, recheck the same-day total across networks and keep it below three.
