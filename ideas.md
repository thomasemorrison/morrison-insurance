# Morrison Insurance — Design Brainstorm

## Approach A — "East Texas Craftsman"

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** American Craftsman / Heritage Editorial

**Core Principles:**
1. Warmth through texture — linen-like backgrounds, wood-grain dividers, aged paper tones
2. Serif authority — editorial headings that feel like a well-read local newspaper
3. Asymmetric storytelling — content blocks that breathe and feel hand-placed, not grid-locked
4. Restrained color — the palette does the heavy lifting, not gradients

**Color Philosophy:**
Deep forest pine green (#1E3A2F) as the dominant authority color. Warm cream (#F5F0E8) as the page ground. Amber gold (#C8922A) as the accent — evoking East Texas sunsets and courthouse brass. Charcoal (#2C2C2C) for body text. The palette should feel like a well-worn leather journal found in a county courthouse.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Full-bleed sections with alternating text-left / image-right compositions. Hero uses a large typographic statement with an offset image card. Navigation is horizontal with a thin rule separator. Sections are separated by subtle diagonal cuts rather than hard lines.

**Signature Elements:**
- Thin horizontal rules with small diamond ornaments between sections
- Sepia-toned photography treatment on hero images
- Pull-quote blocks with oversized quotation marks in gold

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Slow, deliberate hover states — underlines that draw in from left, cards that lift 4px with a soft shadow. Nothing feels rushed. The site should feel like talking to someone who takes their time.

**Animation:**
Fade-up on scroll (60px travel, 500ms ease-out). Section headlines stagger in word by word. Hero text appears with a gentle blur-to-sharp transition. No bouncing, no spinning.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Playfair Display (bold, 700) — authority and heritage
- Body: Source Serif Pro (400/600) — readable, warm, editorial
- UI/Labels: DM Sans (500) — clean contrast to the serif stack
</text>

## Approach B — "Modern Heritage" (SELECTED)

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Modern Heritage / New American Vernacular

**Core Principles:**
1. Confident simplicity — generous whitespace with purposeful typographic hierarchy
2. Local character — color and texture rooted in the East Texas landscape (pine, earth, sky)
3. Trust through restraint — no flashy gradients, no aggressive CTAs; the design itself communicates stability
4. Editorial structure — long-form sections that feel like a trusted local publication, not a sales funnel

**Color Philosophy:**
Deep pine green (oklch(0.28 0.08 155)) as the primary brand color — grounded, natural, authoritative. Warm off-white (oklch(0.97 0.01 80)) as the page background — not sterile white, but warm like natural paper. Warm amber (oklch(0.72 0.12 65)) as the accent — used sparingly for CTAs and highlights. Slate charcoal (oklch(0.22 0.01 250)) for text. The palette should evoke a well-established institution that has been part of the community for a century.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Asymmetric two-column compositions for service sections. Hero uses a full-width typographic statement with a large background image anchored to the right. Navigation is sticky with a thin pine-green underline on active items. Sections alternate between light cream and deep pine backgrounds for rhythm. No centered-everything layouts.

**Signature Elements:**
- A thin horizontal rule in amber gold used as a section opener
- Oversized year numerals (1923) used as a decorative background element in the heritage section
- Service cards with a left-border accent in pine green

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Hover states that feel like a firm handshake — immediate, confident, not flashy. Buttons scale to 0.97 on press. Links get a warm amber underline on hover. Cards lift with a 6px shadow.

**Animation:**
Scroll-triggered fade-up (40px, 400ms, cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)). Hero headline appears with a 200ms stagger per line. Stats counter animates when in viewport. No parallax — it feels gimmicky for a trust-first brand.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Lora (700) — classic serif with warmth and authority, not stuffy
- Body: Inter (400/500) — clean and highly readable for long-form content
- Accent/Labels: Lora italic (400) — used for supporting lines and pull quotes
</text>

## Approach C — "Deep East Texas Broadsheet"

<response>
<text>
**Design Movement:** Neo-Traditional / Broadsheet Revival

**Core Principles:**
1. Typographic density — information-rich layouts that reward reading, like a well-designed magazine
2. Grid discipline — a strict 12-column grid with intentional breaks for visual interest
3. Contrast through scale — massive display type alongside small-cap labels creates hierarchy
4. Community feel — the design should feel like it belongs on a bulletin board at the county courthouse

**Color Philosophy:**
Ink black (#1A1A1A) as the primary text color. Newsprint cream (#F2EDE3) as the background. A single accent color — deep burgundy (#7A1E2E) — used only for key CTAs and the logo mark. The restraint of near-monochrome makes the brand feel established and serious.

**Layout Paradigm:**
Broadsheet-inspired multi-column text layouts for service pages. Hero uses a newspaper front-page composition with a dominant headline and a secondary story column. Navigation uses small-caps with generous letter-spacing.

**Signature Elements:**
- Vertical rule dividers between columns
- Small-cap section labels with tight tracking
- A "masthead" style header with the agency name in large display type

**Interaction Philosophy:**
Minimal animation — this is a serious, trustworthy brand. Hover states are subtle color shifts. The focus is on content, not motion.

**Animation:**
Minimal — only essential fade-ins on page load. No scroll animations. The content speaks for itself.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Libre Baskerville (700) — newspaper authority
- Body: Libre Baskerville (400) — consistent serif throughout
- Labels: Montserrat (600, small-caps) — clean contrast
</text>
