## Purpose & Brand Objectives

This calendar governs all **manually created** social media content for Morrison Insurance. Its goals are:

1. **Build community trust** — position Morrison as a neighbor, not a vendor.
2. **Establish local authority** — be the go-to source for what's happening in Shelby County.
3. **Inform and educate** — help East Texans make better decisions about protecting their families and businesses.
4. **Generate leads** — move prospects from awareness to inquiry through consistent, low-pressure CTAs.
5. **Support SEO** — drive traffic back to morrison-ins.net through links and brand search volume.

Blog articles (auto-posted Wednesday via Metricool RSS) handle the **educational/persuasion** function. This manual calendar handles **community connection, brand personality, and seasonal relevance**.

## Post Formats by Platform

### Facebook
- **Link posts:** Include the article or resource URL in the post body. Facebook auto-generates the preview card.
- **Photo posts:** 1200 × 630 px. Caption up to 300 words. Include a soft CTA in the last line.
- **Engagement posts:** Ask a question. No link. Designed to generate comments.

### Instagram
- **Feed posts:** 1080 × 1080 px square. Caption up to 150 words. Hashtags in first comment or at end of caption.
- **No link in caption** — use "Link in bio" and keep the bio link pointed to morrison-ins.net.

## Post Messaging Principles

Every post — regardless of pillar — follows these rules:

**Voice:** Write like a trusted neighbor who happens to know insurance. Warm, direct, never salesy. Use "we" and "our community." Avoid corporate language.

**CTA approach:** Soft CTAs only on brand posts. Never push for a sale. Acceptable CTAs:
- "Give us a call at (936) 598-2791"
- "We're always happy to answer questions."
- "Come see us at 125 Shelbyville St."
- "Link in bio" (Instagram only)

**Length:**
- Facebook: 50–200 words. Longer is fine for community stories.
- Instagram: 50–125 words. Hashtags at the end or in first comment.

**No fabricated content:** Never invent customer quotes, testimonials, reviews, or statistics. All claims must be verifiable.

**Engagement prompts:** End community posts with a question when natural — "Have you been out on Toledo Bend lately?" or "Did you catch the game Friday night?" This drives comments and organic reach.
