### H2 — Flood insurance is included in your homeowner's policy

There are two ways an insurance policy can pay you after a loss. One pays what your property was worth. The other pays what it costs to replace it.

The difference can be thousands of dollars — and it's usually buried in the fine print.

ACV = Actual Cash Value. What it was worth before the damage.RCV = Replacement Cost Value. What it costs to fix or replace it today.

If you don't know which one you have, it's worth finding out before you need it.
