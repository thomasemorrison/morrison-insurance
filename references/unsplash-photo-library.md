## How to Use This Library

1. Click any photo URL to open it on Unsplash
2. Click **Download free** (no account needed for standard resolution)
3. For Canva: click **Download** → choose size → import directly into your Canva template
4. For blog: download and upload via the Morrison Insurance website media library
