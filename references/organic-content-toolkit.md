# Morrison Insurance — Organic Content Toolkit

---

## Part 1 — 20 Engagement Questions

These are designed to generate comments, shares, and saves. Each one is rooted in East Texas life and connects naturally to the Morrison brand without feeling like a sales pitch. Post one per week in an open slot — Tuesday or Thursday mornings tend to perform best for comment-driving content.

Format: Single image with the question as the text overlay, or a plain text post. Keep the caption short — the question IS the content.

---

### Community & Local Life

**Q1.**
> "What's something about living in East Texas that you'd never trade for anything?"

*Why it works:* Pure pride post. Floods comments with local love and tags. Boostable.

**Q2.**
> "Name a Shelby County business that's been around as long as you can remember. We'll start: Morrison Insurance, since 1923. 👇"

*Why it works:* Celebrates local legacy, invites community participation, subtly reinforces the Morrison story.

**Q3.**
> "What's the best kept secret in Shelby County — a restaurant, a fishing hole, a back road, anything?"

*Why it works:* High shareability. People love sharing local knowledge and tagging friends.

**Q4.**
> "If you had to describe East Texas weather in one word, what would it be?"

*Why it works:* Easy to answer, funny, relatable. Great for a slow news day.

**Q5.**
> "Toledo Bend or Sam Rayburn — where do you spend your weekends?"

*Why it works:* Friendly debate. Drives comments and tags. Perfect for spring/summer.

---

### Home & Property

**Q6.**
> "If your house caught fire and you had 60 seconds to grab three things (family and pets are already safe), what would you take?"

*Why it works:* Emotional, thought-provoking, and directly relevant to home insurance. Drives long, personal responses. One of the highest-performing question formats in insurance.

**Q7.**
> "What's one thing in your home you'd have a hard time replacing if it was stolen or destroyed?"

*Why it works:* Naturally leads to a conversation about endorsements and scheduled coverage — without being salesy.

**Q8.**
> "How long has it been since you actually read your homeowners policy?"

*Why it works:* Honest, a little uncomfortable, relatable. Drives DMs from people who realize they should call.

**Q9.**
> "Do you know off the top of your head what your home deductible is?"

*Why it works:* Most people don't. Creates a gentle moment of awareness without shaming anyone.

**Q10.**
> "What's the biggest storm damage you've ever dealt with on your property?"

*Why it works:* Story-sharing post. People open up about hail, tornadoes, floods. Strong engagement and social proof for why coverage matters.

---

### Vehicles & Recreation

**Q11.**
> "What's sitting in your shop or barn right now that probably isn't insured?"

*Why it works:* Directly connects to the fleet coverage series. Drives comments and quiet DMs from people who realize they have a gap.

**Q12.**
> "First truck or first car — what was it, and how old were you?"

*Why it works:* Nostalgia post. Massive engagement. Connects to the teen driver series without being preachy.

**Q13.**
> "ATV, UTV, side-by-side, or boat — what's your weekend ride?"

*Why it works:* Fun, local, and subtly surfaces the fleet coverage conversation.

**Q14.**
> "What's the longest fishing trip you've ever taken to Toledo Bend? Worth it?"

*Why it works:* Highly local, drives comments from the fishing community, pairs naturally with the weekly fishing report.

**Q15.**
> "Do you know if your boat is covered if it sinks at the dock?"

*Why it works:* Most people don't know the answer. Creates genuine curiosity and drives DMs.

---

### Seasonal & Timely

**Q16.**
> "It's the first cold front of the year. What's the first thing you do — fire up the smoker, pull out the deer stand, or turn on the heat?"

*Why it works:* Perfect for late September/October. Pure East Texas culture. High engagement.

**Q17.**
> "Opening day of deer season — who's already got their stand ready?"

*Why it works:* Seasonal, community-specific, drives comments and tags. Post the Thursday before opening weekend.

**Q18.**
> "Cotton harvest season is here. What does that smell mean to you?"

*Why it works:* Hyper-local sensory post. People who grew up here respond emotionally to this.

**Q19.**
> "Summer in East Texas: the heat, the humidity, the thunderstorms. What's your survival strategy?"

*Why it works:* Relatable, funny, seasonal. Works any time June–August.

**Q20.**
> "What's the best thing about fall in Shelby County?"

*Why it works:* Broad enough for everyone to answer — football, hunting, cooler weather, the trees. High comment volume.

## Part 3 — Local Monitoring Checklist

Check these sources weekly (the Local Intelligence Scan task handles Monday — this is for real-time awareness during the week):

### Daily (2 minutes)
- [ ] **Shelby County Today** (shelbycountytoday.com) — breaking news, accidents, fires, community announcements
- [ ] **Light & Champion** (lightandchampion.com) — local news and events
- [ ] **Shelby County Facebook groups** — "Shelby County Community," "Center TX Community Board," local buy/sell groups often surface news before media does

### Weekly
- [ ] **NWS Shreveport** (weather.gov/shv) — storm outlooks, watches, warnings for Deep East Texas
- [ ] **TCEQ Burn Ban Map** (tceq.texas.gov) — Shelby County burn ban status
- [ ] **Toledo Bend water level** (toledobend.com/level) — note any significant rise/fall
- [ ] **Shelby County website** (co.shelby.tx.us) — official notices, burn bans, commissioner announcements

### As Needed
- [ ] **TPWD fishing reports** — supplement the Thursday fishing report task
- [ ] **Local school district pages** — Center ISD, Shelbyville ISD, Timpson ISD for sports scores and events
- [ ] **Chamber of Commerce** — Center Chamber for business openings, ribbon cuttings (Welcome to Shelby County template)

## Part 4 — Real-Time Post Templates

When something happens locally and you need to post fast, use one of these fill-in frameworks:

**Breaking community news:**
> "[What happened] in [location]. Thinking of everyone affected. If you need help understanding your coverage options, we're here. 📞 (936) 598-2791"

**Burn ban lifted:**
> "Burn ban has been lifted in Shelby County as of [date]. Always check co.shelby.tx.us for the latest updates before you burn. Stay safe out there."
*(Use Burn Update Canva template — update "PROHIBITED" to "LIFTED" and adjust the body copy)*

**Weather event passing:**
> "Storms have moved through. If you've got damage, document everything with photos before cleanup begins, then give us a call. We'll walk you through the claims process. 📞 (936) 598-2791"

**Local win/good news:**
> "[Headline or brief description of the good news]. Proud to be part of this community. 🌲 #ShelbyCounty #EastTexas"
*(Use Good News for Shelby County Canva template)*

**Toledo Bend conditions (from a local source):**
> "Heard from [a friend / one of our customers / a local guide] out on Toledo Bend this week — [brief conditions note]. Fishing Outlook: [X]/5. Full report drops Thursday. 🎣"
