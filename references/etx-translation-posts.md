## Future Post Ideas (Terms to Add)

- **Premium** — What you pay to keep your coverage active
- **Depreciation** — Stuff loses value as it gets older, even if you took good care of it
- **Covered Loss** — Something your policy was written to protect you from
- **Limits** — The most your policy will pay on a covered claim
- **Personal Property** — Your furniture, clothes, appliances, and the stuff inside your home
- **Flood Exclusion** — Standard homeowners policies don't cover flood; that's a separate policy
- **Subrogation** — When your insurance company recovers money from the party that caused your loss
- **Binder** — Temporary proof of coverage while your policy is being finalized
